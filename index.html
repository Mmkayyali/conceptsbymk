019 · Concept 19 of 100

Side B

Listening speakeasy · Publishes Tuesday, November 17, 2026

One record at a time, played whole. Behind an unmarked door at the back of a small record shop: ten seats at a walnut counter, all of them facing a wall of vinyl.

01

The room

Ten seats at a walnut counter facing the vinyl wall, the turntable at its center like an altar, two speakers flanking it.

02

The ice ritual

Halfway down the bar, a block of clear ice sits in a metal lined well, and the blade that carves it rests right in front.

03

The booths

Four low booths in deep teal lacquer for the ones who stay past side two. Voices low. Listening first.
